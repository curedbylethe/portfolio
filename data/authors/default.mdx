*Parsa Jahanbani / Full-stack product engineer*

**I build software for people who rely on it.**

My background is in frontend engineering, particularly interfaces that have to make complex data usable. I also build backend services, write systems software, and operate the applications I build independently.

## Professional work / 2023–2026

I spent three years at Soorin, working on financial software with product, design, and backend colleagues. I helped improve an established trading platform, took over the frontend of a live mobile advisory app, and built the frontend foundations of a new fund-operations platform from scratch.

Maintaining the trading platform taught me what happens when shared state, data contracts, and interface complexity accumulate over time. Starting a new product gave me the opportunity to address those problems in its architecture, from typed state and composable UI primitives to deployment and testing.

## Independent work

Much of my independent work starts with a problem I encounter in my own life or workflow. Sometimes that means writing a small tool; other times it grows into a complete application. These projects give me room to explore areas beyond frontend engineering, including systems programming, backend services, infrastructure, and developer tooling.

I also contribute to the open-source software I use, investigating bugs and working on improvements when I encounter problems in those tools. I document some of my larger projects, including their architecture, decisions, and trade-offs, in the project dossiers.

## Now

I'm available for frontend and full-stack product engineering roles, particularly work involving complex interfaces, application architecture, or performance. I continue to develop my independent projects.
