However, we can also go about solving this the old-fashioned way, using indicators, fodder and definition.

The entire clue is the definition, and it also contains the wordplay. For indicators, we have *spun* (a reversal indicator), *around* (a containing indicator) and *unlimited* (a deletion indicator).

Spin or reverse the word THE and you get EHT. Unlimit, or remove the limits (the ends) of SIGN and you get IG. Now put EHT around IG and you get <Spoiler>eight</Spoiler>.
