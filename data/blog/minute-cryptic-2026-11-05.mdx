Another clue playing with the double meaning of *bear*. *Bear* is a container indicator here and once you see it, the rest becomes easy.

POLAR has 5 letters, missing two letters to meet the answer's number of letters. Next we have *turning up*, with the word UP meeting the two-letter criteria we're looking for and *turning* is a reversal indicator. Putting PU inside POLAR gets us POPULAR, which means in fashion, very cool (and hot!), our definition.
